How the hold is made: HoldingTradeLedger (test-only) wraps the real ledger and
parks the first caller right after its INSERT INTO loan_trade, inside its open
transaction, until the test releases it. The second request is sent only after
the first is parked; the test then polls pg_stat_activity (bounded, 10 s) until
a backend waits on Lock/transactionid, which is the second INSERT blocked on
the first's uncommitted entry in the unique index.

Both scenarios passed 10 repetitions each (@RepeatedTest(10)); the numbers
above are from the first repetition. "sent to answered" includes the time the
test held the first request, so it measures the test, not the ledger. What it
shows is the cost the decision record names: for the whole hold, the second
request occupied a Tomcat worker and a pooled connection while doing nothing.
