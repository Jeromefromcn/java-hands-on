Stage 04 section 2 — bean lifecycle order, measured on Spring Framework 7.0.9
(Spring Boot 4.1.1), JDK 21.0.12, via LifecycleLoggingBeanTest.

Full recorded order, one ApplicationContextRunner context, closed at the end:

  1. postProcessBeanFactory
  2. bpp.beforeInstantiation
  3. constructor
  4. bpp.afterInstantiation
  5. setter injection
  6. setBeanName
  7. bpp.beforeInitialization
  8. @PostConstruct
  9. afterPropertiesSet
  10. initMethod
  11. bpp.afterInitialization
  12. afterSingletonsInstantiated
  13. SmartLifecycle.start
  14. ContextRefreshedEvent
  15. SmartLifecycle.stop
  16. @PreDestroy
  17. destroy
  18. destroyMethod

Hypothesis falsified: the plan predicted @PostConstruct before
bpp.beforeInitialization, since CommonAnnotationBeanPostProcessor is
PriorityOrdered. Measured the opposite (7 before 8).

BeanPostProcessor chain as registered, printed from
AbstractBeanFactory.getBeanPostProcessors() in a context holding only
CallOrderRecorder and RecordingBeanPostProcessor (throwaway probe test, not
kept):

  SPRING 7.0.9
  BPP-ORDER org.springframework.context.support.ApplicationContextAwareProcessor
  BPP-ORDER org.springframework.context.annotation.ConfigurationClassPostProcessor$ImportAwareBeanPostProcessor
  BPP-ORDER org.springframework.context.support.PostProcessorRegistrationDelegate$BeanPostProcessorChecker
  BPP-ORDER com.jerome.slledger.bootstrap.demo.lifecycle.RecordingBeanPostProcessor
  BPP-ORDER org.springframework.context.annotation.CommonAnnotationBeanPostProcessor
  BPP-ORDER org.springframework.beans.factory.annotation.AutowiredAnnotationBeanPostProcessor
  BPP-ORDER org.springframework.context.support.ApplicationListenerDetector

Cause, from spring-context 7.0.9 PostProcessorRegistrationDelegate
.registerBeanPostProcessors: PriorityOrdered BPPs are registered first, but
any that are also MergedBeanDefinitionPostProcessor are collected into
internalPostProcessors and, after the plain BPPs, re-registered with
"// Finally, re-register all internal BeanPostProcessors."
AbstractBeanFactory.addBeanPostProcessor removes an existing entry and
appends it to the end, so CommonAnnotationBeanPostProcessor (@PostConstruct)
and AutowiredAnnotationBeanPostProcessor (@Autowired) end up behind every
plain BPP.
