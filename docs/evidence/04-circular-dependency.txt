ApplicationContextRunner does not follow Spring Framework's default here.
spring-boot-test 4.1.1 AbstractApplicationContextRunner keeps
`private boolean allowCircularReferences;` (uninitialised, so false) and
applies it with `autowireCapableBeanFactory.setAllowCircularReferences(...)`
— it mirrors Spring Boot's policy. Plain Spring behaviour needs
`withAllowCircularReferences(true)`.

↑     ↓

Action:

Relying upon circular references is discouraged and they are prohibited by default. Update your application to remove the dependency cycle between beans. As a last resort, it may be possible to break the cycle automatically by setting spring.main.allow-circular-references to true.
