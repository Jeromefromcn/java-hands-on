=== Profile activation ===

$ ./mvnw help:active-profiles -pl .
 - local (source: com.jerome.slledger:sl-ledger-parent:0.1.0-SNAPSHOT)

$ ./mvnw help:active-profiles -pl . -Pci
 - ci (source: com.jerome.slledger:sl-ledger-parent:0.1.0-SNAPSHOT)

$ CI=true ./mvnw help:active-profiles -pl .
 - ci (source: com.jerome.slledger:sl-ledger-parent:0.1.0-SNAPSHOT)

# Note: activating ci removes local. activeByDefault is switched off as
# soon as any other profile in the same POM activates, so the two are
# mutually exclusive without anything saying so.
