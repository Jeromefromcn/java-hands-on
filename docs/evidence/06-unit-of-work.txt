theSameSequenceIsRefusedAtTheStaleWrite (OptimisticLoanPositionRepository):

Why the spec's Propagation.MANDATORY would not have caught it: T3 is a real,
active transaction. MANDATORY asks "is there a transaction", and the answer
is yes. The question that matters is "did this transaction load this
instance", and that is what the identity map answers.
