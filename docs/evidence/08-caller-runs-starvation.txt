read-timeout=0, a source that hangs every call. Four GET /quotes requests (one ISIN
each) are sent, then GET /loans with a 2 s client timeout.

1. policy=caller-runs.
   Requests that reached the source: 3 (1 on the market-data worker, 2 on Tomcat

   Tomcat workers whose stack contains RestMarketPriceSource.priceOf: [http-nio-auto-2-exec-1, http-nio-auto-2-exec-3]
   (their own request threads, executing the task the executor refused.)


2. policy=abort, same setup.
   Two quotes answered 503 (283 ms after sending); two are still waiting on the source.
